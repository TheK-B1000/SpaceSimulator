# Star Sparrow (third-party)

Source: Spaceship Modular Star Sparrow, free promotional sample from Ebal Studios on Fab.

Listing: https://www.fab.com/listings/932ce79b-06e8-416a-ba0d-36435621a0a1

This repository keeps only:

- `Meshes/Examples/SM_StarSparrow01`
- `Materials/MI_StarSparrow_Red` and its `Materials/Common` dependencies
- Shared `Textures/`

Unused examples, modular parts, demo maps, and Blueprints are gitignored. Re-add the Fab pack locally if you need them.

Textures in this free sample are 2K. Do not use for extreme close-ups. The pack is not designed for AI usage per the Fab listing.
